1. DPA Overview
This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between 1S Enterprises LLC ("Data Processor" / "Provider") and the Client identified in the applicable Service Order ("Data Controller" / "Client"). This DPA forms part of, and is subject to, the Terms of Service between the parties.
This DPA governs the processing of personal data by the Provider on behalf of the Client in connection with the delivery of cloud infrastructure services. It satisfies the requirements of GDPR Article 28 and applicable US state privacy laws.
2. Scope & Roles
Provider acts as a Data Processor — processing personal data only on documented instructions from the Client. Client acts as Data Controller — determining the purposes and means of personal data processing. Provider will not process personal data for any purpose other than providing the contracted Services, unless required by applicable law.
3. Processing Activities
Nature of Processing
  • Storage and transmission of data within client-provisioned VM environments
  • Monitoring of system metrics (CPU, memory, disk — no application-layer data)
  • Automated snapshot creation and cross-region replication of VM disk data
  • Access logging for security and compliance purposes
Categories of Data Subjects
End users of Client's applications or services whose data is stored in the provisioned environment, as determined solely by the Client.
Categories of Personal Data
As determined by the Client. Provider has no visibility into the content of Client's data and does not inspect, analyse, or process application-level data.
4. Technical & Organisational Security Measures
  • AES-256 encryption of all data at rest on Persistent Disk
  • TLS 1.3 encryption of all data in transit
  • Shielded VM boot with vTPM and Secure Boot enabled
  • VPC isolation with least-privilege firewall rules
  • MFA required for all Provider personnel access
  • Cloud Audit Logs retained for 400 days
  • Annual penetration testing by qualified third party
  • Monthly OS security patching with critical CVE <24hr response (Enterprise)
  • Physical security: Google Cloud data centre controls (ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II certified)

6. International Data Transfers
All Client data is processed and stored in Google Cloud us-east1 (South Carolina, USA). No data is transferred outside the United States without explicit written consent from the Client. For EU/EEA clients, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs, 2021/914) are incorporated by reference and available upon request.
7. Business Associate Agreement (HIPAA)
For clients that are HIPAA Covered Entities or Business Associates storing Protected Health Information (PHI) in their provisioned environment, 1S Enterprises LLC executes a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) as required by 45 CFR §164.308.
BAA Commitments
  • PHI will not be used or disclosed other than as permitted by the BAA and required by law
  • Appropriate technical, physical, and administrative safeguards will be maintained
  • Subcontractors (Google Cloud) are bound by equivalent BAA obligations
  • PHI breaches will be reported to Client within 72 hours of discovery
  • Upon termination, PHI in Provider-controlled systems will be deleted within 30 days
Note: The BAA covers Provider's management activities only. Client remains solely responsible for the HIPAA compliance of applications and workflows running within their VM environment.
8. Execution
This DPA is incorporated into the Service Agreement upon execution of a Service Order. A countersigned PDF copy is provided to all clients. For Enterprise clients requiring custom DPA terms, please contact legal@1senterprisesllc.com.

1. Definitions
"Uptime" means the percentage of time in a calendar month during which Client's provisioned VMs are accessible and operational, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and Excluded Events. "Downtime" means any period where VM accessibility falls below the SLA threshold, measured in minutes. "Monthly Uptime Percentage" = ((total minutes − downtime minutes) / total minutes) × 100.

5. Exclusions
The following are excluded from uptime calculations and do not constitute SLA breaches:
  • Scheduled maintenance windows (communicated 72+ hours in advance)
  • Client-initiated actions (shutdown, reboot, misconfiguration)
  • Force majeure events (natural disaster, acts of war, government actions)
  • Google Cloud Platform-wide incidents affecting all customers equally
  • Network issues outside Google Cloud's network boundary
  • Suspension of services due to non-payment or acceptable use violations
6. Measurement & Verification
Uptime is measured using Google Cloud Monitoring synthetic monitors with checks every 60 seconds from three independent geographic locations. Raw monitoring data is available to Enterprise clients in real time via a shared Cloud Monitoring dashboard. Downtime is measured in minutes and is verifiable against Cloud Audit Logs.
7. SLA Reporting
Enterprise clients receive a monthly SLA compliance report by the 5th business day of the following month, covering: monthly uptime percentage, total downtime minutes, incident log with root cause analysis, patch compliance status, and next-month maintenance schedule. Professional clients receive quarterly summaries. SLA reports are delivered via email and archived in the client's Slack channel.
